Workflow

From satellite pass to sprinkler schedule

  1. 01

    Pick your field

    Search your farm on satellite imagery and draw its boundary — or upload a screenshot if you already have one.

  2. 02

    Let the pixels split it

    A vegetation index runs over the image, and K-Means with elbow detection decides how many zones your field actually needs.

    Water only what’s dry
